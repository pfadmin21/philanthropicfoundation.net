What We Do

A platform that makes it easier
for capital to reach Africa

Most capital that could reach Africa doesn't — not because the opportunities aren't there, but because the institutional infrastructure is missing. The governance frameworks, the trusted relationships with regulators and DFIs, the convening platforms where the right people actually meet.

The Philanthropic Foundation was built to fill that gap. We are a Mauritius-registered, FSC-supervised institution that operates two programmes — a regulated climate investment fund and Africa's only pan-continental financial conference circuit — both designed to move capital more efficiently across the continent.

We are not a bank, a consultancy, or a development agency. We are a governance and programme platform with real institutional relationships — DFIs, embassies, sovereign wealth contacts, and fund managers — built over years, not assembled for a pitch deck.

How It Works
01 — Governance
The Foundation holds the institutional layer
Registered in Mauritius and supervised by the FSC, the Foundation provides the governance architecture — legal structure, DFI relationships, regulatory standing — that its programmes operate within.
02 — Deployment
Programmes deploy into specific opportunities
Each programme has a focused mandate: the MCTF deploys blended finance into African climate infrastructure. The African Circuit convenes the investors, policymakers, and capital allocators who make deals happen.
03 — Compounding
Capital flows more efficiently as a result
By combining a regulated investment vehicle with a continent-wide convening platform, the Foundation creates compounding value — the relationships built at the Circuit feed the Fund's pipeline, and vice versa.

Blended Finance · FSC-Regulated CIS · Est. 2021
Mauritius Climate Transition Fund
A closed-end, FSC-regulated collective investment scheme that deploys blended finance into climate infrastructure across Africa and the Indian Ocean. The Fund stacks DFI concessional debt beneath institutional equity to create bankable project structures that European pension funds and asset managers can access — without compromising on governance standards. Targeted at renewable energy, water, coastal resilience, and low-carbon logistics.

Five invitation-only events per year across Mauritius, Nairobi, Cape Town, Casablanca, and online — bringing together fund managers, DFI limited partners, government ministers, UHNWI principals, and institutional investors in the same room. Who We Work With

The Foundation works with institutions
that are serious about Africa

Our programmes are designed for a specific audience — institutions that understand emerging markets, value governance, and are ready to deploy or engage at scale.

Development Finance Institutions
DFIs are co-investors in the MCTF and delegates at The African Circuit. Our founding relationships give us direct access to their LP networks and co-financing facilities.
Institutional Investors
Pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, and ESG-mandated asset managers looking for governed, bankable exposure to African infrastructure and capital markets.
Governments & Regulators
Ministers, central bank officials, and regulators participate at Circuit events and support the MCTF's project pipeline through policy dialogue and concessions.
Sponsors & Strategic Partners
Financial services firms, law firms, banks, and corporates who want sustained visibility across Africa's most relevant institutional audiences — not one-off conferences.
